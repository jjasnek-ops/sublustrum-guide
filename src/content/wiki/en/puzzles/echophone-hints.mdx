## What does the Echophone establish?

An Echophone response is a clue to investigate, not a complete answer. In the October 2 development notes, the team explains that the device is intended to aid exploration. It also acknowledges earlier uncertainty about whether a player had found the right area or was operating the device correctly.

The published changes include larger detection areas for certain objects, more noticeable prompts, and clearer visual and audio feedback. Those statements do not provide an exact detection radius or a map of every detectable item.

## How much guidance do you want?

The panels are closed by default. These are observation methods grounded in the developer’s description, rather than a hidden numeric solution or a guaranteed object location.

## Does silence mean the game is broken?

Silence alone does not establish a bug. The developer’s notes show that detection feedback needed refinement, but an unanswered puzzle can also reflect a missed clue or an interaction you have not yet understood. Describe the reproducible behavior before labeling it a defect.
