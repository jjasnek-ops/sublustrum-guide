## What should I check before starting?

Confirm that you installed the remake playtest, rather than finding a walkthrough for the 2008 original. [Steam access instructions](/updates/playtest/) explain the October testing window. Compare your PC with the [official requirements](/updates/system-requirements/). The advertised performance targets are not a benchmark of your machine.

The developer says the test covers Chapter One. A trailer can show areas outside that playable slice. Do not plan your session around an assumed full campaign or a route copied from Outcry.

## How can I keep useful exploration notes?

Use a short observation log. Record what you see and hear before deciding what a mechanism means. These are suggested session habits, not a verified room-by-room route.

| Record | Useful detail |
| --- | --- |
| Your location | A room name visible in the game, or a clear description |
| Object or document | The visible label and the clue you actually read |
| Action | What you changed, and its starting state |
| Feedback | A sound, prompt, light or movement after the action |
| Open question | What you still need to establish |

Changing one thing at a time makes it easier to separate a clue from an unrelated sound. Revisit a document using the inventory UI when a mechanism appears to refer to it. The developer’s October 2 notes describe revised inventory scrolling and document navigation; exact bindings should be checked in your installed build.

## What if an interaction seems to do nothing?

Look for both visual and audio feedback, and compare the result with your notes. The developer acknowledges that some earlier interactions did not communicate a successful action clearly enough. That history makes it worth documenting an apparent blocker, but it does not establish that every unsolved puzzle is a bug.

For device-specific context, open [Echophone hints](/puzzles/echophone-hints/). Avoid repeatedly applying an old numeric answer: the [original-versus-remake comparison](/guides/original-vs-remake/) explains why that can mislead you.

## How do I report a useful blocker?

Include your build if visible, hardware, location, actions already tried and what you expected to happen. Distinguish “the prompt never appears” from “I do not know the solution.” Add a screenshot or a short recording if the official feedback channel accepts it. Mark puzzle details as spoilers.

The developer links a feedback form inside the playtest and directs puzzle conversations to the [playtest discussion hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4191280/discussions/). This fan guide does not operate that hub.
