## Is Sublustrum the same game as Outcry?

The developer’s July 29 FAQ calls the original Sublustrum the game many players know as **Outcry**. That connection explains why searches return older walkthroughs. It does not make an original-game solution a verified answer for the remake on Steam app 4151300.

## What does the remake rebuild?

| Area | Original described in the official FAQ | Remake described in the official FAQ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploration | Point-and-click, fixed screens | Freely explored 3D locations |
| Spaces | Individual scenes and viewpoints | Interconnected spaces, new perspectives and expanded areas |
| Puzzles | Original mechanics | Familiar mechanics refined with new details |
| Identity | Original concept and atmosphere | Those foundations preserved within a rebuilt experience |

The developer describes a reimagining, rather than a scene-by-scene recreation. The official website also describes audio-driven puzzles and a photoreal 3D world. Promotional descriptions of the whole game do not establish which expanded areas are accessible in Chapter One.

## What remains from the original?

The July FAQ confirms Anthesteria remains part of the remake’s musical identity. It also says the team intends to bring the original game to Steam, while focusing on the remake. An intention to release the original is not proof of a separate, currently purchasable original edition.

## Can I reuse a numeric code from an older walkthrough?

Verify it in the actual build first. The team explicitly says puzzles are being refined, and the October test follows another set of changes. An older instrument can look familiar while its interaction or required context differs. This page intentionally contains no legacy numeric solutions.
