## When is the full Sublustrum remake coming out?

The current [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4151300/Sublustrum/) lists **Q1 2027**. That means the first quarter of 2027, a window spanning January through March rather than a confirmed launch day. The product is marked coming soon.

Older announcements and search snippets can still refer to a 2026 release plan. For current timing, prefer the live store listing. We have not established when the store field changed, so this page does not invent a delay-announcement date or the reason for the change.

## Why can people play it in October 2026?

The public **playtest** is a separate Chapter One build. Its announced testing window is October 12–25, 2026. Playing that slice does not mean the completed campaign has been released. [Playtest access](/updates/playtest/) covers enrollment and scope; [getting started](/guides/getting-started/) covers preparation.

## Who is making the remake?

Steam currently names **Brickworks Games** as developer and **HypeTrain Digital** as publisher. The October 12 official announcement also describes their partnership. The original game’s studio history is separate from those current store credits; see [original versus remake](/guides/original-vs-remake/).

## How can I check whether this changed?

Check the release field on app 4151300 and its official news. If a specific day replaces the quarter, record the source and update the verification date. A full-release walkthrough should also be checked against the released build before reusing playtest steps.
