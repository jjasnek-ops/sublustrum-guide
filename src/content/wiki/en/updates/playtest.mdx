## How do I get into the Steam playtest?

1. Open the [Sublustrum Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4151300/Sublustrum/) and confirm app **4151300**.
2. Sign into the Steam account you intend to use.
3. Find the playtest section and use **Request Access**.
4. Once Steam grants access, find **Sublustrum Playtest** in your library and install it.

If you made a request during the closed test, check your account and library rather than assuming that an old request guarantees current installation access. We have not tested approval speed on a fresh account, and the announcement does not establish a guaranteed waiting time.

## What is playable, and for how long?

The October 5 announcement schedules an updated first chapter for **October 12–25, 2026**. The October 12 launch announcement confirms that the public test opened and says it runs until October 25. Neither establishes a precise cutoff hour or timezone.

The launch description places the opening in the Professor’s home, where you investigate traces and restore machinery. It does not publish a full route. Trailers and store screenshots can illustrate the wider remake; they are not proof that every shown location belongs to this test.

## What changed after the closed test?

The developers say they revised controls, environmental interactions, puzzles, audio and the interface. The October 2 feedback notes discuss device response and inventory usability in more detail. Our [Echophone guide](/puzzles/echophone-hints/) explains the supported device information without publishing unverified solutions.

## What did the October 16 playtest fixes change?

The developers report five fixes in the current playtest build:

- The English diary voice-over now finishes its final words.
- Inspecting items on low graphics settings no longer produces an unintended red glow.
- Steam Deck gamepad detection and cutscene playback were corrected.
- Notes no longer show a next-page hint on their final page.
- Starting a new game from the main menu now warns that existing progress will reset.

These are the changes stated in the [October 16 official announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4151300/view/1846651706681508). The Steam Deck fixes do not establish a Steam Deck Verified rating. The announcement supplies no puzzle combinations or revised solution steps.

## Where should I send feedback?

Use the short survey linked directly in the game. For a reproducible issue, state the location, build if visible and actions taken. The developer directs puzzle discussions to the [playtest forum](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4191280/discussions/), and links its community from official announcements. Mark spoilers before describing a solution.
